# Client reporting

## Daily report
The client receives a daily report per office pulled from the canvassing app at the end of each day. It lists attempts (labeled "doors" in the client's sheet) and contacts as submitted, before any QC rollback. We do not edit a daily report after it is sent.

## Weekly report format
Every Monday we send the client one table for the week that ended Sunday, one row per office plus a total: doors, contacts, registration cards, and paid hours. Doors and contacts use our dashboard definitions (doors once per day, rollbacks removed). Cards are valid registration cards, duplicates removed. Hours are paid hours for GOTV and registration shifts together; shifts still missing a clock out are left out and noted.

## Explaining differences
When the weekly report and the client's daily sheets disagree, explain the difference as repeat attempts plus rolled-back attempts, with the dates and offices where rollbacks happened.
