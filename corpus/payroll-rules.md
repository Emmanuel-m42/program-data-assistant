# Payroll rules

## Pay rates
Canvassers are paid hourly by role: canvasser, field lead, registration canvasser and registration lead. Rates are in the pay rates table. Paid hours are clock in to clock out for each shift.

## Overtime
The overtime threshold is 40 paid hours in a payroll week, Monday through Sunday, counting every shift the person worked. Hours over 40 are paid at 1.5 times the hourly rate. Any shift that takes a person over 40 hours needs the field director's approval and stays pending until it is approved.

## Shifts that need fixing
A shift marked needs_fix cannot be paid until a field lead corrects it. Common reasons are a missing clock out (no paid hours yet) and a shift clocked under the wrong office. Fix the record, then mark it approved.

## Payroll deadline
Payroll for the week that ended Sunday runs Tuesday at noon. Every shift must be approved by Monday at 5 PM; pending and needs_fix shifts left after that roll to the next payroll.

## Cost per door
Cost per door is GOTV wages (paid hours times the hourly rate for the person's role) divided by doors for the same office and period. Overtime premiums are left out of this figure.
